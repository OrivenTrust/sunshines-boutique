Our story

A little shop with a lot of light

Sunshine's Boutique was born in Namugongo from a simple wish: that every woman should feel radiant in what she wears.

We hunt for pieces that are beautiful, wearable and kind to your budget — then we style them, photograph them and share them with you first. Come for a dress, stay for the warm welcome.
