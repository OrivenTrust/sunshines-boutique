Privacy

Your information, kept kindly

Sunshine's Boutique is a small fashion boutique in Namugongo, Kampala, Uganda. This page explains what we collect through sunshinesboutique.com and our app, and how we use it. Last updated 1 October 2026.

What we collect

  • Account details — if you create an account (with email or Google), we keep your name, email address and profile photo from Google, and any phone number you choose to add.
  • Wishlist and reviews — the pieces you save, and any rating or review you post. Reviews show your first name publicly.
  • Messages — what you send through our contact form, with the email or phone you give us so we can reply.

How we use it

Only to run the shop: to let you sign in, keep your favourites, reply to your messages and orders, and show reviews. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. Orders placed over WhatsApp are handled in WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored securely with our service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in), Google (sign-in with Google), Resend (delivering message notifications to us) and GitHub Pages (hosting the website).

Your choices

You can edit your details or delete your review at any time from your account page. To have your account and data deleted entirely, email us at ajoklilian027@gmail.com and we'll do it promptly.
